ALL ABOUT ME

I am a passionate full-stack web developer with a deliberate emphasis on Backend Engineering, software architecture, and distributed systems. My craft is anchored in developing reliable digital infrastructures where data security, optimized queries, and clean OOP abstractions meet seamlessly.
